Why We Exist

Bears for Learning was created by people who learn differently and were tired of being mocked, rushed, or dismissed when we asked for help. So we built the place we always wished existed — a den where you can say "I don't understand" without fear. Mama bears protect their cubs. That's what we do here.

What to Expect

  • You can ask "obvious" questions — they're welcome here>
  • You can ask the same thing twice, or five
  • Nobody will make you feel small
  • Help at your pace in whatever way clicks for you
  • Kindness is the only rule that matters
